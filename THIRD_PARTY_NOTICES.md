## GT-B substrate prediction

The residue-property constants and feature-based comparator follow Hennen et al.,
[Decoding substrate specificity determining factors in glycosyltransferase-B
enzymes](https://doi.org/10.1039/D4DD00338A).

- Source: [GTB_substrate_prediction](https://github.com/vbharadwcomosci/GTB_substrate_prediction)
- Revision: bb6b16c6bea4d6987bfa388edb77f734d00eb445
- Copyright (c) 2025 Vivek S. Bharadwaj
- License: [MIT](licenses/GTB_substrate_prediction.txt)
- Data: [Zenodo release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15786055)

This project adapts the feature-based method to single-label prediction and
grouped development evaluation. The MIT copyright and permission notice is
retained in full.
